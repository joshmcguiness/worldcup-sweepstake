How this works
A computer simulation plays out the whole tournament tens of thousands of times. Each match is decided by the teams’ betting strength (tempered so upsets happen at a realistic rate), group games draw about 24% of the time, the top two plus the eight best third-placed teams advance, and the real bracket is then played out. Real results always override the simulation as they come in.

Odds of a Player Winning

What the betting markets imply: each team’s tournament-winner odds, combined across all of a player’s teams, then normalised so the whole pool adds up to 100%. This is the chance one of your teams lifts the trophy.

📐 Model vs Market

What the predictive model actually is
• Every team gets a strength rating from its tournament-winner odds (live bookmaker prices), tempered with a cube root so single-game upsets happen at realistic rates.
• A match between two teams is a Bradley-Terry coin flip weighted by those ratings; group games draw ~24% of the time (the long-run World Cup rate).
• 30,000 full-tournament simulations per refresh play out the groups, best-thirds and the real bracket to get progression and champion probabilities. Real results lock in as they land.
• Edge below = model probability × bookmaker odds − 1. Positive means the books are paying more than the model thinks the outcome is worth; the "Implied" column is the market's own probability with the bookmaker margin stripped out.

Calibration — when the model says X%, how often does it actually happen?

The model's core weakness: probabilities are squeezed toward the middle. It over-rates longshots and under-rates near-certainties — so low-probability calls lose money at the model's own prices, while its "65–80%" calls are actually bankers.

Day-by-day P/L

🎲 High Risk Curnow Bets

Two books of five calls every day, found by the model from the day's fixtures, the 30,000-run simulation, FIFA rank gaps and the Golden Boot odds board: Best bets that finalise today (double chances, result singles, anytime scorers, scorer-plus-win combos and the multi — highest probability first, settled by tonight) and Longer bets (group crowns, deep runs, outrights — settled over the tournament). The model only prices what it can actually measure — team strength, progression odds and striker quality — so no invented markets. Picks lock each morning, settle automatically, and every call carries a virtual $100 stake so we can track how the model's tipping really performs.
